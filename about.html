About Dr. Siamak Darehbaghi

I earned my medical degree from Midwestern University in Arizona and completed residency training in Psychiatry at LewisGale Medical Center, in affiliation with Virginia Tech and the Veterans Affairs Health System.

I am board certified in Psychiatry and licensed in New York State. My academic experience includes serving as an Associate Professor of Clinical Psychiatry at Albert Einstein School of Medicine, where I taught, mentored, and contributed to academic leadership.

My approach is warm, collaborative, and evidence-based. I help adults navigate anxiety, depression, mood disorders, ADHD, trauma, psychotic disorders, sleep disorders, and life transitions.
